What's driving markets

  • Rate hikes are coming. October CPI was hot (headline +0.4%, core +0.3% for the 3rd straight month). Prediction markets price an ~87% chance of a 25bp hike on Dec 9 (~10% for 50bp), with more possible into early 2027. Fed funds would go from 3.75–4.00% toward ~4.4%.
  • AI stocks in correction. Worries about AI companies borrowing heavily for data centers, fears that the Democratic midterm sweep limits data-center construction, and Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO (Nov 12). Nasdaq fell 5.5% on Nov 5; Nvidia is down 6 straight weeks.
  • Gold and crypto falling with real yields. With bonds paying 5%, assets that pay nothing lose appeal, and the strong dollar adds pressure.

The forward P/E is low because analysts expect very strong earnings: +33% in 2026 and +16% in 2027, much of it tied to AI spending. If data-center spending slows (policy limits, financing pressure), those estimates fall and the "cheap" P/E becomes less cheap.
Verdict: valuations are reasonable, not cheap. They support buying gradually, not making a big leveraged bet.

Why not buy them right now

  • Expensive: 27% (SPY) and 33% (QQQ) implied volatility for 2-year options is high; calm markets are typically high-teens to low-20s. 5% rates also raise call prices because the option price includes ~2 years of financing cost.
  • Big downside in a correction: estimated that if SPY fell 15% over 6 months, the at-the-money SPY LEAP would lose about 40–50%, breaking WealthEngine's 15% drawdown guard.
  • QQQM options are too illiquid: ~$5 bid-ask spread on a ~$70 option (7%) and only 25–58 contracts of open interest. Use QQQ for Nasdaq LEAPS.
  • Timing: Nvidia (~8% of the S&P, more of the Nasdaq) reports Nov 17, the Fed meets Dec 9, and QQQ sits right on its 200-day average.

If you still want LEAPS

  • Choose SPY over QQQ: healthier trend, less AI concentration.
  • Use deep in-the-money strikes (~80% of price, ~0.80 delta): less of the price is decaying time value.
  • Keep it to 5–10% of the portfolio, bought in 2–3 tranches after Dec 9.
  • Cheaper alternative: a LEAPS bull call spread (buy one call, sell a higher one). Costs less and is less hurt by high implied volatility.
