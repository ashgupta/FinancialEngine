### Key Insight
Total loss is identical ($240,900) regardless of down payment. The loss is driven by price decline ($210K + $31K selling costs), not financing structure. With 10% down, more of the loss is realized as cash needed at closing ($114K vs $47K) because the larger loan means you're deeper underwater.
